# 💡 Vibe Coding Prompt Inspiration

Ready to level up PoopTunes? Copy and paste these prompts into your favorite AI agent (like Gemini, Claude, or ChatGPT) to start expanding the app.

## 🌈 Aesthetics & UI
- **Glassmorphism**: *"Update the track cards and player bar to use a glassmorphism effect with a subtle blur and a 1px border that glows when hovered."*
- **Ambient Background**: *"Create a background animation for the app shell that features slow-moving, organic blobs in shades of brown and gold (#8b4a1f to #e8c56a)."*
- **Custom Scrollbar**: *"Make the scrollbar in the library grid super thin and colored like the amber accent, with a smooth fade-in when scrolling."*

## 🎵 Audio Features
- **Visualizer**: *"Add a Canvas-based frequency visualizer to the Player component that sits behind the track title and reacts to the audio levels."*
- **Crossfade**: *"Implement a 3-second crossfade between songs when I skip to the next track."*
- **Lo-fi Filter**: *"Add a 'Lo-fi' toggle button that applies a low-pass filter and adds a subtle crackle sound effect to whatever is playing."*

## 📁 Management & UX
- **Search**: *"Add a search bar at the top of the LibraryGrid that filters tracks by title or artist in real-time."*
- **Playlists**: *"Implement a way to create a 'Pooplist'. I want to be able to click a '+' icon on a track to add it to a custom collection."*
- **Album Art**: *"Update the upload system to automatically extract and display the high-resolution album art from the MP3's ID3 tags."*

## 🤖 Advanced AI Integrations
- **Vibe Description**: *"When I upload a song, use an LLM to analyze the title/artist and generate a 1-sentence 'vibe description' (e.g. 'Perfect for a rainy afternoon in a coffee shop')."*
- **Smart Shuffle**: *"Create a shuffle mode that groups songs together based on their tempo or mood metadata."*

## 🚀 DevOps & Deployment
- **Mobile App**: *"Help me wrap this project using Capacitor so I can run PoopTunes as a native app on my iPhone."*
- **Cloud Sync**: *"Add an option to backup my SQLite database to a private S3 bucket every night at 3 AM."*

---

**Tip**: When prompting, always mention that you want to maintain the **"Black & Brown premium vibe"** and that all styles should go into `src/index.css`.
